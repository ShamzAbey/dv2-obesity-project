Data sources used in this project

1. Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS), National Health Survey: State and territory findings, 2022.
   Used for adult overweight/obesity, overweight, obesity and physical activity guideline rates by state and territory.
   URL: https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/health/health-conditions-and-risks/national-health-survey-state-and-territory-findings/2022

2. Australian Institute of Health and Welfare (AIHW), Overweight and obesity, 2024.
   Used for the age/sex trend framing; values are from the ABS 2022 NHS BMI table cited by AIHW for Figure 1.
   URL: https://www.aihw.gov.au/reports/overweight-obesity/overweight-and-obesity/contents/summary

3. Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS), National Health Survey, 2022.
   Used as the official survey source for national BMI Table 8.3 proportions and physical activity definitions and tables.
   URL: https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/health/health-conditions-and-risks/national-health-survey/2022

4. Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS), Apparent Consumption of Selected Foodstuffs, Australia, 2023-24 financial year.
   Used for selected non-alcoholic beverage apparent consumption, 2018-19 to 2023-24.
   URL: https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/health/food-and-nutrition/apparent-consumption-selected-foodstuffs-australia/latest-release

5. Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS), Dietary Behaviour, 2022.
   Used for adult fruit and vegetable recommendation rates by age group.
   URL: https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/health/health-conditions-and-risks/dietary-behaviour/2022

6. Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS), National, state and territory population, June 2022.
   Used for state/territory estimated resident population in the physical activity scatter plot point sizing.
   URL: https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/population/national-state-and-territory-population/jun-2022

7. cartdeco/Australia-json-data, Australian TopoJSON geometry.
   Used only for local Australian state/territory map geometry. Health rates are joined from CSV files above.
   URL: https://github.com/cartdeco/Australia-json-data

8. Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS), Average Weekly Earnings, Australia.
   Used for state-level income context in the correlation matrix.
   URL: https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/labour/earnings-and-working-conditions/average-weekly-earnings-australia/latest-release

Notes:
- All percentages are proportions reported by ABS/AIHW and are manually transcribed from the public tables/text listed above.
- state_health_profile.csv combines ABS state obesity, weight status, activity, population, beverage and earnings context into one profile table for the bubble plot and dashboard.
- sugar_sweetened_state_2017.csv uses ABS 2017-18 state/territory daily sugar-sweetened drink consumption by sex. This is used as the available unhealthy food/drink geography measure.
- state_diet_2022.csv uses ABS 2022 state/territory fruit and vegetable recommendation rates.
- weight_status_time_state.csv and weight_status_time_lookup.csv use ABS state/territory adults overweight or obese rates from 2007-08, 2011-12, 2014-15, 2017-18 and 2022.
- No direct public state-level fast-food or takeaway-meal frequency dataset was added. A future extension should add that specific dataset if available; this project does not fabricate fast-food rates.
- correlation_matrix.csv is calculated from the state_health_profile.csv variables.
- radar_profile.csv, radar_axes.csv and parallel_profile_long.csv reshape state_health_profile.csv for advanced Vega-Lite profile views.
- age_behaviour_heatmap.csv combines ABS BMI age rates with ABS Dietary Behaviour age rates.
- obesity_gender.csv and obesity_age.csv use adult age groups from ABS Table 8.3 Body Mass Index proportions.
- obesity_age_annotation.csv contains a text-only annotation derived from obesity_age.csv.
- lifestyle_risk_index.csv is a derived index calculated from ABS adult obesity rates and the share not meeting physical activity guidelines.
- lifestyle_risk_bands.csv contains display bands for the derived lifestyle risk index chart.
- adult_diet_recommendations.csv uses ABS Dietary Behaviour 2022 fruit and vegetable recommendation values by adult age group.
- state_locations.csv contains state and territory capital coordinates used only to place proportional symbols on the map.
- state_tile_cartogram.csv arranges ABS state and territory overweight values into a manually positioned tile cartogram.
- food_treemap.csv contains a manually calculated treemap layout derived from ABS 2023-24 beverage consumption values.
- sankey_nodes.csv and sankey_links.csv group ABS state obesity and physical activity values into display categories for a Vega-Lite Sankey-style alluvial diagram.
- demographic_node_link_nodes.csv and demographic_node_link_edges.csv arrange ABS age and gender obesity values into a Vega-Lite node-link layout.
- demographic_dendrogram_nodes.csv and demographic_dendrogram_links.csv arrange the same ABS age and gender values into a Vega-Lite dendrogram layout.
- australia_states.topojson is geometry only and does not contain obesity, physical activity or food consumption values.
